# About QICP Equality

## Project Overview

**QICP Equality (QICP)** is a sovereign, open-source economic inclusion platform architected natively for the **Internet Computer Protocol (ICP)**. 

### Mission & Core Beneficiaries
Our foundational mandate is structural economic empowerment for historically marginalized and vulnerable communities worldwide:
- **Moolnivasi (Indigenous & tribal populations)**
- **Scheduled Castes (SC)**
- **Scheduled Tribes (ST)**
- **Other Backward Classes (OBC)**
- **Religious, Linguistic, and Ethnic Minorities**
- **Unbanked, informal, and rural workers globally**

Through transparent, on-chain public goods grants, vocational livelihood assistance, and social micro-enterprise funding, QICP provides censorship-resistant financial infrastructure without middlemen or extractive fees.

2. **NIST Post-Quantum Cryptography**: Quantum-resistant lattice cryptography adhering to official NIST FIPS 204 (ML-DSA-65) and NIST FIPS 203 (ML-KEM-768) specifications.
3. **Strategic Elastic Tokenomics**: Theoretical supply ceiling is mathematically uncapped, tied strictly to verified public goods generation and counterbalanced by speculative demurrage decay and x402 bazaar fee burns.
4. **Deterministic Conway Automaton**: Cellular lattice consensus evaluating peer density and deriving grant velocity multipliers without pseudo-random simulation.
5. **RFC HTTP 402 Bazaar Protocol**: Micro-payment monetization rail for decentralized AI model reasoning and resource access.
