# Mission & Socio-Economic Inclusion

## 1. Core Commitment

The **QICP Equality Protocol** is dedicated to advancing structural economic sovereignty and inclusion for historically marginalized populations:
- **Moolnivasi (Indigenous communities)**
- **Scheduled Castes (SC)**
- **Scheduled Tribes (ST)**
- **Other Backward Classes (OBC)**
- **Religious, Linguistic, and Ethnic Minorities**
- **Economically Vulnerable & Underserved Communities Worldwide**

---

## 2. Guiding Principles

1. **Grassroots Livelihoods First**: Protocol grants prioritize tangible vocational tools, agricultural inputs, clean water public goods, and social micro-enterprises.
2. **Zero Plutocracy & Hoarding Resistance**: Wealth accumulation through idle speculation is counteracted by algorithmic demurrage decay ($0.5\%$ monthly on idle balances exceeding 1M QICP).
3. **Quadratic Inclusion Match**: Allocations scale with the square root of verified participants ($\sqrt{N}$), empowering large grassroots federations while neutralizing sybil exploitation.
4. **No Financial Returns or Profit Promises**: QICP tokens are utility public goods for community exchange and resource access. The protocol never promises guaranteed returns, interest yield, or passive dividends.
